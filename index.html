Pecking Order

Who rules the roost?

A new daily ranking puzzle from Puzzl

How to play

Put five things in order, top to bottom. Today's question tells you what you're ranking them by.

Drag a card to move it, or tap two cards to swap them. When you're happy, press Lock it in.

Cards in the right place turn green and stay put. The rest you can keep moving.

You get three tries, one for each egg. When the game ends you'll see the real numbers.

Everyone gets the same puzzle. A new one arrives every day at 7am UK time.
